# Read A Paper Pertinent to your Final Exam Project

*Purpose: The purpose of this homework assignment is to help you start to think about your final exam project and explore some of the existing datsets in your field. If you would prefer to use one of the “suggested” datasets, they are listed below.*

## Select a Paper that will relate to your FINAL PROJECT

  * Think about what project you would like to do for your final exam project. It can be relevant to your own field or come from a list of Suggested Projects (below).
- **Briefly summarize** the point of the paper (you don't need to read the whole paper, just get a gist of what the paper is about through the abstract and by skimming)
- Briefly summarize the approach the researchers used in their RNA-seq analysis.
  - You'll probably need to read the materials & methods section for this, or the STAR protocols, or a supplemental method
  - Report what software and versions were used?
  - Report Is this Paired end or single end?
  - Report whether the researchers include information on the data repository

**! NOTE:** The pipeline we are using works on paired-end sequencing. Please try to identify a dataset that uses paired-end sequencing. 

**! NOTE:** microarray, ddPCR, minION, and pacBIO datasets will not work for this assignment.

#### HINTS:
  
  *  If you are going to use your OWN data for your final project and that data is not yet published,you can either find a publication in the same organism OR a similar type of experiment done in another field.
